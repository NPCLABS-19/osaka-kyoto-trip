Garden of Fine Arts

09:00–17:00 · last entry 16:30

Azuma House

Private home · exterior only

A brief, discreet observation from the public street. Never approach the door or photograph residents.

Sumiyoshi Taisha

10–15 min onward from Azuma

A calm optional add-on, not a second obligation. Skip it freely if energy or weather says so.

SEP 25 · OSAKA

WAD Omotenashi Café

12:00–19:00 · cash · walk-in

Modern tea service in white-walled calm. No reservations and irregular closures, so recheck close to the date.

SEP 26 · IKOMA

Ikoma Sanjo / Sky Cycle

Park free · Sky Cycle ¥600 pp

Take the scenic ride first. Wind, rain, queues and 40-minute cable gaps are the real ground conditions.

Image source ↗Official / map ↗
SEP 27 OR SEP 25

B.B. / American Village

About 11:00–22:00 · no appointment

The easiest item to move. Online comments flag high pricing; browse first and verify before purchasing.

Grand Green Osaka

Public realm · mostly open-air

A low-stakes Umeda park walk near the rail corridor. The wider Umekita development completes in stages through 2027.
