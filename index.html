SEP 22 · ARRIVAL GATEKyoto by 09:45 unlocks Inoichi.

Arrive later and skip the ticket queue. Keep the Garden or a central walk, collect your luggage, and continue to Osaka without rushing.

SEP 23 · WEATHER SWITCHBad visibility cancels Ikoma.

Use covered Umeda and Grand Green instead. Ikoma is closed on Sep 24 and Sep 25, so there is no safe full-day reschedule.
